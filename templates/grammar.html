📚 IELTS Grammar: Oson va Tushunarli Qoidalar

IELTS imtihonida yuqori ball (Band 7+) olish uchun eng muhim grammatik qoidalar. Har bir mavzu o'zbek tilida oddiy tilda tushuntirilgan.

Band 6.5+ Talabi

1. Passive Voice (Majhul Nisbat)

IELTS yozish (Writing) qismida faqat "Men qildim" (Active) emas, balki "Bu qilindi" (Passive) deb yozish ballni keskin oshiradi.

💡 Formula: Subject + to be (am/is/are/was/were) + V3 (Fe'lning 3-shakli)

❌ Oddiy: People grow coffee in Brazil.

✅ IELTS usuli (Passive): Coffee is grown in Brazil.

Gap tuzilishini boyitish

2. Relative Clauses (Who, Which, Where, Whose)

Ikkita qisqa gapni bitta uzun va chiroyli murakkab gapga bog'lash uchun ishlatiladi.

💡 Asosiy bog'lovchilar: Who (odamlar uchun), Which/That (narsalar uchun), Where (joy uchun).

❌ Oddiy: I met a teacher. He taught me English.

✅ IELTS usuli: I met a teacher who taught me English.

Shart gaplar

3. Second & Third Conditionals (Faraziy gaplar)

Speaking Part 3 da o'z fikringizni "Agar shunday bo'lganida edi..." deb tushuntirishda juda qo'l keladi.

💡 Formula (2-turi): If + Past Simple, would + V1

✅ Misol: If governments invested more in education, crime rates would decrease.
